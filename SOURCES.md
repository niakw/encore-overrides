# Sources

## Nintendo Switch 1 hardware

Nintendo official specifications:

- NVIDIA custom Tegra processor.
- Built-in display: 1280×720.
- TV output: up to 1920×1080 at 60 FPS.

https://www.nintendo.com/fr-fr/Hardware/Gamme-Nintendo-Switch/Nintendo-Switch/Nintendo-Switch-1148779.html

Public technical references for the Tegra-based Switch platform also document 4 GB LPDDR4-class memory and the Maxwell-derived GPU configuration. These figures are useful context, but Nintendo's official page above remains the primary source for the console's declared output capabilities.

## EA SPORTS FC on Switch 1

### EA SPORTS FC 24

Nintendo Life reports EA/IGN confirmation that FC 24 moved to Frostbite on Switch and runs at 30 FPS.

A later graphics comparison reports:

- 1080p docked
- 720p handheld
- 30 FPS in both modes

https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2023/09/ea-sports-fc-24-switch-frame-rate-and-resolution-revealed

https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2023/10/video-ea-sports-fc-24-graphics-comparison-shows-off-switch-performance-and-resolution

### EA SPORTS FC 25

Nintendo Life's Switch review reports the Frostbite version remains at 30 FPS.

https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch/ea-sports-fc-25

### EA SPORTS FC 27

Nintendo officially lists a Nintendo Switch release dated 25 September 2026.

https://www.nintendo.com/fr-fr/Jeux/Jeux-Nintendo-Switch/EA-SPORTS-FC-27-3147152.html

At the time this candidate was authored, no sufficiently trustworthy independent pixel-count measurement for **FC 27 on Switch 1** had been accepted into this repository.

Therefore:

- FC 27 Switch 1 is **not** claimed here to render at an exact resolution.
- The 1080p/30 FC 24 data is used only as the documented Frostbite-era Switch baseline.
- FC 25's 30 FPS result supports continuity of that performance target.
- Any future direct FC 27 measurement supersedes the inference.
